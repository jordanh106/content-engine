# Cinema Studio 2.0 Defaults for Chiropractic Content

Use these defaults unless a specific video plan says otherwise.

## Medical/Educational Content (Formats A, D)
- **Camera:** ARRI Alexa (warm, professional)
- **Lens:** Cooke (character-driven bokeh)
- **Focal Length:** 35mm (natural narrative)
- **Genre:** Intimate
- **Color:** Warm, inviting, teal (#0d9488) accents

## Anatomical Visualizations
- **Camera:** RED V-RAPTOR (sharp, detailed)
- **Lens:** Zeiss Ultra Prime (neutral)
- **Focal Length:** 50mm (tight focus)
- **Genre:** Auto
- **Color:** Teal glow on dark background, medical-professional

## Exercise/Movement Demos (Format C)
- **Camera:** Sony Venice (clean, sharp)
- **Lens:** Canon K35 (bright, warm)
- **Focal Length:** 24mm (full body visible)
- **Genre:** Intimate
- **Color:** Bright, clean, wellness aesthetic

## Treatment Walkthroughs (Format E)
- **Camera:** ARRI Alexa (warm, professional)

- **Focal Length:** 35mm
- **Genre:** Intimate
- **Color:** Warm clinical setting, reassuring tones

## Myth Busters (Format D)
