## 1. Hook Architecture (First 0-3 Seconds)

Top-performing chiropractic videos share four hook traits. A compliant script must include at least 3 of these 4.

### Required Components

| # | Component | Description | Compliance Check |
|---|-----------|-------------|------------------|
| 1 | **Visual Hook** | What viewers SEE first. Pattern interrupt, anatomy diagram, before/after, demo motion, or face-direct shocking expression. | Script must include a `[Visual: ...]` cue in seconds 0-3 |
| 2 | **Text Hook** | BOLD on-screen text overlay (60-80% screen height, white with black outline, high contrast). | Script must include a `[Text overlay: ...]` cue in seconds 0-3 |
| 3 | **Spoken Hook** | What you SAY. Pattern-interrupt phrase, statistic, or question opening a curiosity gap. | First spoken line under 12 words, ends in unresolved tension |
| 4 | **Audio Hook** | Sound/music supporting tone — trending audio, sound effect, or intentional silence. | Script must specify audio (`[Audio: ...]` or `[SFX: ...]`) at second 0 |

### Top-Performing Hook Archetypes (Use One)

| Archetype | Example | Best Format | Optimizes |
|-----------|---------|-------------|-----------|
| **Pattern Interrupt** | "Hard pill to swallow: Your back pain isn't from the gym. It's from your chair." | D, F | Shares, comments |
| **Statistic Stop** | "80% of desk workers have forward head posture. Watch this." | A, F | Saves |
| **Question Curiosity** | "Why does your back hurt every morning?" | A, B | Watch time |
| **Demonstration Open** | "Watch what happens when I adjust this." | C, E | Watch time |
| **Contrarian Snapback** | "Stretching your neck is making it worse. Here's why." | D | Shares |
| **Identity Hook** | "If you're a [pregnant mom / desk worker / parent], you need to see this." | F, G | Saves |

### Hook Compliance Rules

- First spoken line: **≤ 12 words**
- Text overlay: **bold sans-serif, white + black outline, 50-60% screen height**
- Pattern interrupt or curiosity gap **MUST resolve unanswered by second 5**
- NO weak openers: "Hey guys," "Today I want to talk about," "So..."
- NO buried lead — payoff teased in hook, not delivered until after Re-hook 1

## 2. Body Structure (Re-Hook Architecture)

Top retention comes from stacking curiosity loops, not linear delivery.

### The Required Structure

```
HOOK (0-3s)
  ↓
CONTEXT (3-6s) — set the stakes
  ↓
CONFLICT (6-12s) — present the problem
  ↓
RE-HOOK 1 (12-15s) — "But here's the twist..." / "What most people don't know..."
  ↓
CONTEXT 2 (15-22s) — deliver the unexpected fact
  ↓
CONFLICT 2 (22-28s) — show the consequence
  ↓
RE-HOOK 2 (28-32s, only for 30s+ videos) — "And there's one more thing..."
  ↓
PAYOFF (final 5-8s) — the answer + CTA
```

### Body Compliance Rules

- **At least one re-hook** for any video over 20s
- **No more than one re-hook** for videos under 20s (avoid choppiness)
- Each re-hook must include a contrast word: **but, actually, except, however, the truth is**
- Pacing: scene/visual change every **2-3 seconds** for TikTok, **3-5 seconds** for Reels
- Educational core must be delivered between Re-hook 1 and Re-hook 2 (or before Payoff)

## 3. Visual & Production Standards

### Required Shot Elements

| Element | Specification | Compliance Check |
|---------|---------------|------------------|
| **Talking-head shot** | Eye-level, well-lit, professional setting | Present in 70%+ of scenes |
| **B-roll / demonstration** | Anatomy graphic, technique demo, or visual metaphor | At least 1 cut to B-roll within first 10s |
| **On-screen text** | Reinforces spoken word, NOT verbatim transcript | Text appears within 1s of new scene |
| **Pattern-interrupt cuts** | Hard cuts, zoom-ins, or angle changes | Cut every 2-4s; no static shots > 4s |
| **Color grading** | Warm, natural, NOT clinical-blue | Color grade specified in production notes |

### Cinema Studio Defaults (per content type)

| Content Type | Camera | Lens | Focal Length | Genre |
|--------------|--------|------|-------------|-------|
| Educational/Talking | ARRI Alexa | Cooke | 35mm | Intimate |
| Anatomical/Visual | RED V-RAPTOR | Zeiss Ultra Prime | 50mm | Auto |
| Demo/Movement | Sony Venice | Canon K35 | 24mm | Intimate |

### Production Compliance Rules

- **Audio:** clean, no echo, music ≤ -18 dB under voiceover
- **Captions:** burned-in, max 2 lines, white with black outline
- **Aspect ratio:** 9:16 for Reels/TikTok/Shorts, 16:9 only for YouTube long-form
- **Duration:** match format target (A: 30-45s, B: 30-45s, C: 30-60s, D: 15-30s, E: 45-60s, F: 6-15s, G: 15-30s)

## 4. CTA & Engagement Mechanics

### Required Engagement Elements

| Element | Description | When |
|---------|-------------|------|
| **Save trigger** | Phrase: "Save this for [moment]" — "next time you wake up sore," "your next desk break," etc. | All F (Quick Tip), B (Checklist) formats |
| **Share trigger** | "Send this to someone who [audience trait]" | All G (Patient Story), D (Myth) formats |
| **Comment prompt** | Open-ended question or controversial stance invitation | All D (Myth), A (Explainer) formats |
| **Profile follow** | "Follow @collectivechiro for more [topic-specific] content" | End card on all videos |
| **DM keyword** | "Comment '[KEYWORD]' to get [resource]" — drives DM funnel | High-intent videos only (B, F) |

### CTA Compliance Rules

- One PRIMARY CTA per video (save OR share OR comment, not all three)
- CTA appears **after the educational payoff**, never before
- Tone: empowering, not begging. "Save this for your next desk break" not "Please save and share!"
- Profile follow happens via end card visual, not spoken (avoids feeling like an ad)

## 8. Anti-Patterns (Hard Fails)

The compliance gate **rejects** any script with these. They tank performance every time.

| Anti-Pattern | Why It Fails | Replacement |
|--------------|-------------|-------------|
| "Hey guys, today I want to talk about..." | Weak opener, 90%+ skip in first 2s | Direct hook + visual + text overlay |
| Generic "what is X" without stakes | No curiosity gap | Open with a consequence or surprise |
| Solving 3 problems in one video | Cognitive overload, no save trigger | One problem, one solution, one save trigger |
| Static talking head > 4s without B-roll | Visual fatigue, scroll drop | Cut to anatomy / demo / text card every 2-3s |
| Apologizing or hedging ("Sorry, this might be wrong, but...") | Erodes authority | Confident statements only |
| Begging CTAs ("PLEASE like and share!") | Pushes audience away | Earned CTAs tied to value |
| Emdashes in script text | Violates brand voice rule | Use commas, periods, or restructure |
| Selling-mode CTA in first half | Triggers skip | All CTAs after Re-hook 1 |
| Identical hook across multiple posts | Algorithm penalty for repetition | Rotate hook archetypes every post |
