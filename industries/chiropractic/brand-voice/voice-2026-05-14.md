# Brand Voice — 2026-05-14

## Style block (the prompt-ready 5-bullet guide)

- Warm, plain, friendly. Like an editor explaining something curious to a friend.
- No emdashes. Use commas, periods, or sentence fragments instead.
- Concrete and specific. Show, do not tell. Avoid jargon and marketing speak.
- Organize by life stage when relevant. Treat families as the organizing unit, not isolated patients.
- Let genuine humor emerge naturally from the material itself. Deadpan asides, audience acknowledgment, relatable reactions. Never force it. Never undercut serious moments.
