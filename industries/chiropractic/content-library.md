## 1. The Strategy

### Why This Works

Every condition your practice treats is a video waiting to be made. A parent searching "signs my baby has colic" at 2am deserves to find your face, your voice, and your expertise. The problem has never been ideas. It's production time.

Cinema Studio 2.0 changes the math. A 30-60 second educational video that would take a full filming session (find the patient, set up the shot, get consent, film multiple takes, edit) now takes 20-30 minutes: record voiceover on your phone, generate visuals in Cinema Studio, assemble in CapCut, publish.

That means you can realistically produce 3-5 videos in a single evening session. Over a month, that's 12-20 new pieces of content without ever stepping in front of a camera.

### The Dual-Purpose Model

Every video in this library serves two purposes:

**1. Social Media Growth**
Short-form content (30-60s) optimized for Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts. These are discovery engines. A parent who finds your "5 Signs Your Baby Has Retained Reflexes" video becomes a follower, then a patient.

**2. Patient Resource**
The same video becomes a shareable resource. When a mom brings in her colicky baby, you can text her the colic explainer video before she even gets home. When a desk worker comes in with tech neck, share the exercise demo they can do at their workstation. The video does the educating so you can focus on the adjusting.

### Production Math

| Metric | Per Video | Per Evening (3-5 videos) | Per Month (8 sessions) |
|--------|-----------|--------------------------|------------------------|
| Voiceover recording | 3-5 min | 15-25 min | 2-3 hours |
| Cinema Studio generation | 10-15 min | 30-75 min | 4-10 hours |
| CapCut assembly | 5-10 min | 15-50 min | 2-6 hours |
| **Total** | **20-30 min** | **1-2.5 hours** | **8-20 hours** |
| **Videos produced** | 1 | 3-5 | **24-40 videos** |

At that pace, this entire 50+ video library can be produced in 6-8 weeks of evening sessions.

### How This Connects to Real-Footage Content

These AI-generated videos don't replace your filmed content. They complement it. Your content calendar already targets 4-5 Reels per week. The split looks like this:

- **2-3 per week:** Real footage (your face, your office, patient interactions, adjustments)
- **2-3 per week:** AI-generated educational content from this library

The real footage builds the personal connection. The AI content scales your educational authority. Together, they make you omnipresent in your niche.

## 2. Reusable Video Format Templates

Five repeatable structures. Swap the condition, keep the format. Every video in the library uses one of these templates.

---

### Format A: "What Is [Condition]?" (The Explainer)

**Duration:** 30-45 seconds
**Best for:** Education, awareness, SEO discovery
**Platform sweet spot:** YouTube Shorts (educational hooks perform best)

**Structure:**
1. **Hook** (0-3s): Question or surprising statement that stops the scroll
2. **What's happening** (3-15s): Simple explanation with Cinema Studio anatomical or lifestyle visual
3. **Why it matters** (15-25s): The consequence of ignoring it, or the connection most people miss
4. **What you can do** (25-35s): Brief actionable step or "here's how we help"
5. **CTA** (35-45s): Follow for more, share with someone who needs this

**Cinema Studio Rig:**
- Camera: ARRI Alexa (warm, professional)
- Lens: Cooke (character-driven bokeh)
- Focal Length: 35mm
- Genre: Intimate
- Shots: 3-4 shots, 2-3 seconds each

**Vibe Motion Graphics:**
- Title card with condition name (teal #0d9488 on dark background, serif font)
- One stat card if relevant ("affects 1 in 4 babies" style)

**Voiceover template:**
> [Hook question or statement]
> Here's what's actually happening. [Simple explanation using everyday language, not clinical terms]
> And here's why that matters. [Consequence or connection]
> [What they can do about it / how chiropractic helps]
> If this sounds like you [or your child], share this with someone who needs to hear it.

## 5. Batch Production Strategy

### The Batch-by-Category Method

Don't produce videos one at a time. Batch by condition category. When you record voiceover for one colic video, your voice is already in that headspace, so record all the infant videos in that sitting. When Cinema Studio is generating one pregnancy visual, queue the next one while you work on assembly.

**Session structure:**

| Phase | What You Do | Time |
|-------|-------------|------|
| **Voiceover Night** | Record all scripts for one category (8-10 videos) | 45-60 min |
| **Generation Night** | Generate all Cinema Studio shots for that category | 60-90 min (mostly waiting) |
| **Assembly Night** | Import to CapCut, sync audio, add captions, export | 60-90 min |

Three evenings = one entire category (8-10 videos) done.

### Recommended Production Order

Start with the categories that will have the most immediate impact on patient intake and social growth:

| Priority | Category | Videos | Why First |
|----------|----------|--------|-----------|
| 1 | Babies & Infants | 10 | Highest emotional engagement, most shareable, biggest growth driver |
| 2 | Pregnancy & Postpartum | 9 | Strong niche authority, high search volume, referral magnet |

## 7. Social Media Distribution

### Posting Cadence

| Platform | Frequency | Content Mix |
|----------|-----------|-------------|
| **Instagram Reels** | 4-5 per week | 2-3 AI-generated (from this library) + 2 real-footage (from content calendar) |
| **YouTube Shorts** | 2-3 per week | Cross-posted from Reels with optimized descriptions |
| **Instagram Stories** | Daily | Behind-the-scenes of production, polls about which video to make next, Q&A |
| **YouTube Long-form** | 1 per week | Expanded versions of popular shorts, or compilation videos by category |

### Platform-Specific Optimization

**Instagram Reels:**
- Checklist formats (Format B) and Myth Busters (Format D) perform best: saves and shares
- Add trending audio when it fits naturally (don't force it)
- First frame should have text overlay matching the hook line
- Captions always on, teal (#0d9488) keyword highlights
- Hashtag strategy: 3-5 niche hashtags (e.g., #pediatricchiropractic #infantcolic #retainedreflexes) + 2-3 broader ones (#chiropractor #familywellness #newmom)

**YouTube Shorts:**
- Explainers (Format A) and Exercises (Format C) perform best: educational search intent
- Descriptions matter for search: include condition keywords, symptoms, and relevant questions people search
- Example description: "What is colic and how can chiropractic help? If your baby cries for hours every day and nothing seems to help, this video explains what's happening in their nervous system and how gentle chiropractic adjustments can provide relief. #colic #infantchiropractic #newborncare"
- First frame is auto-selected as thumbnail, so make shot 1 visually compelling

### Format-to-Platform Performance Map

| Format | Best Platform | Why |
|--------|--------------|-----|
| **A (Explainer)** | YouTube Shorts | Educational hooks match search intent |
| **B (Checklist)** | Instagram Reels | Checklists drive saves and shares |
| **C (Exercise)** | Both equally | Saves well everywhere, high return-viewer rate |
| **D (Myth Buster)** | Instagram Reels | Controversy drives comments and shares |
| **E (Walkthrough)** | YouTube Shorts | "What to expect" is a search-driven query |

### How AI Videos and Real-Footage Videos Complement Each Other

Your content feed should feel like a conversation between two content types:

- **Monday:** AI-generated explainer (educational authority)
- **Tuesday:** Real footage from your office (personal connection)
- **Wednesday:** AI-generated checklist or exercise (shareable value)
- **Thursday:** Real footage patient interaction or behind-the-scenes (trust building)
- **Friday:** AI-generated myth buster (engagement and comments)

The AI content scales your reach. The real content deepens the relationship. Neither replaces the other. Together, they build an audience that trusts you, learns from you, and eventually walks through your door.
