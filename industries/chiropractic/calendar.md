## Weekly Filming Session

**When:** One 30-45 minute block (morning light preferred)

### What to Shoot Each Week

| # | Clip | For | Duration |
|---|------|-----|----------|
| 1 | You delivering Monday's tip/explainer | Mon Reel | 15-30s |
| 2 | You busting Tuesday's myth | Tue Reel | 15-20s |
| 3 | You demonstrating Thursday's exercise | Thu Reel | 10-15s |
| 4 | Walking through a section of the office | Fri Reel + B-roll | 5-10s x 3-4 clips |
| 5 | Detail shots (spine model, equipment, table, hands) | B-roll library | 3-5s x 5-6 clips |
| 6 | Team moment or behind-the-scenes | Fri Reel or Stories | 5-10s |
| 7 | You delivering YouTube main teaching segments | Fri YT Long-form | 1-3 min x 3-4 segments |

**During the week (capture as they happen):**
| # | Clip | For | Duration |
|---|------|-----|----------|
| 8 | Patient reactions post-adjustment (with consent) | Wed Reel | 3-10s |
| 9 | Patient milestone moments (with consent) | Wed Reel or Stories | 5-10s |
| 10 | Any spontaneous team/culture moments | Stories or Fri Reel | 3-10s |

**Total: ~15-25 raw clips per week**

### Post-Filming AI Enhancement Session (1-2 hours)

1. Upload all clips to Mixed Media, apply your home preset (Clean/Premium, teal)
2. Pick 2-3 best static shots, run through Cinema Studio for camera upgrades (Trick 2)
3. Pick 1-2 clips for scene extension if applicable (Trick 1)
4. Generate any Vibe Motion graphics needed (stat cards, quote cards, chapter titles)
5. Generate any AI supplemental inserts needed (spine visualizations for YouTube)
6. Assemble, caption, and schedule

## Cross-Posting Strategy

### Repurposes Well (IG Reel to YT Short)
- You on camera: tips, myths, education (#1)
- Exercise demos (#3)
- Patient moments (#4)
- Trending reactions (#9)
- Vibe Motion stat/quote cards (#12-16)

### Platform-Exclusive
- **Instagram only:** Stories (behind-the-scenes polls, Q&A), carousel posts
- **YouTube only:** Long-form educational (5-10 min), channel trailer, content with chapter cards

### Adaptation
- **Reel to Short:** Usually works as-is. May add a longer intro hook.
- **Reel to YouTube Long-form:** Expand the Reel topic into 5-10 minutes. Use the Reel as a teaser.
- **YouTube Long to Reel:** Pull the single best 15-30 second moment. Add "Full video on YouTube" CTA.

## Performance Tracking

### Key Metrics

**Instagram:**
- Reel views (steady growth week over week)
- Saves and shares (your audience telling you what's valuable)
- Profile visits from Reels (conversion to followers)
- DMs and comments (community building, potential patients)

**YouTube:**
- Watch time (the #1 metric YouTube prioritizes)
- Click-through rate on thumbnails (target: 4-10%)
- Subscriber growth per video
- Shorts views (discovery metric)

### Monthly Review

1. **Top 3 performers** - What made them work? Film more like these.
2. **Bottom 3** - Why didn't they land? Adjust or retire the format.
3. **Most saved/shared** - Your audience telling you what they need most.
4. **YouTube retention** - Where do viewers drop off? Tighten those sections.
5. **Which real footage gets the strongest response?** - Double down on those moments.
