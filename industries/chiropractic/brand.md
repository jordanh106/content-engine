# Collective Family Chiropractic | Content Voice

## Who We Serve

Whole families across every life stage. We are a family-first, whole-body practice with specialties in prenatal and pediatric care.

- Newborns and infants (days/weeks old)
- Toddlers and young children
- School-age kids
- Teenagers
- Adults (desk workers, active adults, everyday life)
- Athletes and active adults
- Prenatal moms (all trimesters)
- Postpartum moms
- Seniors (grandparents, aging well)

A family often comes in together: mom, dad, kids, and sometimes grandparents.

## Brand Voice

Warm, educational, empowering for patient-facing content. Never clinical, cold, or condescending.

**Tone mix:** 70% compelling information, 20% personality/humor, 10% actionable takeaways.

## Jordan's Humor Style

Jordan is naturally funny. The humor is never forced, never scripted comedy. It comes from genuine reactions, deadpan observations, and saying what the audience is already thinking.

- **Deadpan asides** - Brief, dry commentary almost to yourself ("Yeah." / "I'll give them that.")
- **Audience acknowledgment** - Saying what everyone is thinking ("trust your instincts" / "doesn't make me feel that much better")
- **Relatable reactions** - Genuine awe or disbelief
- **Warm callbacks** - Humor that carries real admiration
- **CTA as comedy** - Subscribe asks are punchlines, not begs

### What NOT to Do
- Don't mug for the camera or do exaggerated reactions
- Don't add sound effects or meme overlays to humor moments
- Don't break storytelling flow for a joke
- Don't undercut genuinely heavy or emotional moments

## Content Rules

- No emdashes anywhere in generated content. Use commas, periods, or restructure.
- Patient-facing content uses "you/your" language, never clinical third-person
- Exercise instructions always include frequency ("do this 3 times a day") and difficulty level
- When listing conditions, organize by life stage (not by body region alone)
- Serious setups earn the humor. Humor earns trust. Trust earns the takeaway.
