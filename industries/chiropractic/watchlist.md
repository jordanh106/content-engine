## How to Use

1. **Run full analysis** on priority creators: `/creator-analysis @mommaschiro` (start with prenatal niche, closest to your specialty)
2. **Run landscape scan** quarterly: `/competitor-research chiropractic` to find new creators
3. **Scout viral content** weekly: `/viral-scout chiropractic` before planning content
4. **Review insights** in `creator-insights/<handle>.md` after each analysis
5. **Extract patterns** into the Hook Pattern Library and Idea Bank
6. **Rotate creators** every quarter. Drop inactive accounts, add rising ones

### Priority Analysis Order

Run `/creator-analysis` on these first (highest learning value for Collective Family):

## What to Track

- **Hook patterns:** What type of hooks do they use? Question, myth, statistic, story?
- **Format preferences:** Short tips vs. long explainers vs. demos?
- **Posting cadence:** How often, which platforms, what time?
- **Engagement signals:** Which of their videos get the most saves/shares/comments?
- **Content gaps:** What topics or audiences are they NOT covering that we can own?
- **Visual style:** Editing pace, text overlays, color grading, music choices?

## Notes

- Never copy content directly. Adapt patterns to our brand voice and audience.
- Local competitors are tracked for market awareness, not for content copying.
- National/niche creators are tracked for strategy and format inspiration.
- Track 10-20 creators max. Quality analysis beats quantity.
- Current count: 12 creators across 4 categories.
