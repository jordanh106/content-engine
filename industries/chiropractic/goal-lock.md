# Locked Goal — Q3 2026 (July–September)

**Become the go-to prenatal chiropractor on Instagram for Woodstock, GA and SE United States.**

The outcome is *authority + audience growth* — not raw revenue. Conversions follow once the segment trusts us as the resource. The audience anchor is **prenatal** (Pregnancy & Postpartum) specifically; everything else is downstream momentum.

## Target metrics (by Sept 30, 2026)

| Metric | Target | Why it matters |
|--------|--------|----------------|
| Targeted prenatal Instagram followers | 10,000+ | Authority requires reach in the segment. Local-anchored, not generic. |
| Save rate on prenatal Reels | 7%+ | Saves are the strongest "I want to come back to this" signal. Niche save rates above 7% indicate genuine resource status. |
| Share rate on prenatal Reels | 1.5%+ | Shares with a friend = social proof carry. Strong leading indicator of word-of-mouth growth. |
| Prenatal DMs / week | 8+ | DMs asking about Webster / safety / first visit / pelvic alignment = bottom-funnel intent. The signal that authority is converting to consideration. |
| Booked consultations / month from Instagram | 25+ | The bridge from authority to revenue. Stretch target — measures whether reach is turning into action. |
| Google "prenatal chiropractor Woodstock GA" ranking | Top 3 | Local SEO trails Instagram authority. If we're winning IG, this lifts with it. |

## Sub-targets (so we know we're on pace mid-quarter)

By end of July: 4,000 prenatal followers · 5%+ save rate established · 15 consults from IG · 5 DMs/week
By end of August: 7,000 prenatal followers · 6%+ save rate · 20 consults · 6-7 DMs/week

## Filters every output must pass

Every script, carousel, caption, Reel, or idea generated this quarter has to clear these three filters before it ships:

1. **Audience filter.** Does this speak to a prenatal-segment fear, search term, save pattern, or DM question? (Reference: `industries/chiropractic/audiences.md` § "Pregnancy & Postpartum")
2. **Action filter.** Does this drive toward a save → DM → consult flow, not just a view? Vanity views don't move the needle.
3. **Region filter.** Is this discoverable / shareable by women in Woodstock, GA and SE United States? Generic content gets generic followers; locally-anchored content gets the ones we can actually serve.

If a piece doesn't clear all three, either reframe it or shelve it for a different quarter.

## What "off-topic" looks like

These will tempt you but pull authority away from prenatal:

- Adult tech-neck content (different segment, dilutes focus)
- Generic family chiropractic content not anchored to pregnancy
- Brand-tangential entertainment (e.g. chainsaw history carousel — fun but doesn't book consults)
- Athlete content unless it's specifically "running through pregnancy" or "returning to lifting postpartum"

If you find yourself drawn to one of these, use `/push-back` to find the prenatal-segment version of the angle instead of shelving the idea entirely.

## How the system uses this goal

- **IdeaRanker** reads this file at startup and adds a `goalAlignment` score (0–100) to every ranked idea. Ideas scoring below 40 get demoted unless explicitly flagged for a different objective.
- **Weekly Studio chain** (Sunday 8pm) generates 10 audience-tagged idea cards — at least 6 of the 10 must score `goalAlignment` ≥ 70.
- **`/the-bridge`** biases hook rewrites toward prenatal-audience archetypes from `audiences.md`.
- **`/audience-gaps`** weights silent-question discovery toward consult-booking-friction questions.
- **`/series-planner`** prefers series concepts where Part 1 is prenatal-anchored.
