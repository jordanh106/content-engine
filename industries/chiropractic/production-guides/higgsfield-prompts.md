# Content Production Templates
### Collective Family Chiropractic | Film Real. Enhance with AI.

---

## How to Use This Document

Every template follows the same structure:

1. **FILM THIS** - What to shoot on your phone (the real footage)
2. **ENHANCE WITH** - Which AI tool and prompt upgrades it
3. **VFX TRICK** - Which technique applies (Scene Extension, Camera Upgrade, Environment Enhancement)
4. **SCRIPTS** - Ready-to-use speaking scripts where applicable

Templates are organized in 3 tiers:
- **Tier 1 (80% of posts):** Hybrid content. Real footage + AI enhancement.
- **Tier 2 (15% of posts):** AI graphics. Animated stats, quotes, infographics (Vibe Motion).
- **Tier 3 (5% of posts):** AI supplemental. Things you can't film (spine visualizations, abstract B-roll).

## 1. You Talking to Camera (Tips, Education, Myths)

The anchor of your content. Your face, your voice, your expertise. AI just makes it look polished.

### FILM THIS:
- Stand or sit in your office with natural window light hitting you at a 45-degree angle
- Look directly into the lens. Phone at eye level (prop it on a shelf or use a small tripod)
- Speak naturally, 15-45 seconds per take. Don't worry about perfection; energy matters more.
- Keep a clean, uncluttered background (a teal accent wall or clean treatment room works perfectly)

### ENHANCE WITH:

**Mixed Media** (every clip):
```
Preset: Clean/Premium
Primary Color: Teal (#0d9488)
FPS: 30
```
This gives every talking-head clip the same warm, professional feel.

**Cinema Studio** (for hero content):
Upload your best talking-head clip and apply "Slow Push In" camera preset. Turns a static phone shot into a cinematic, slowly tightening frame that draws the viewer in.

### READY-TO-USE SCRIPTS

**"3 Things I Wish Every Patient Knew" (30s):**
> Three things I wish every patient knew. One: your spine protects your nervous system, which controls everything in your body. Two: pain is usually the last symptom to show up, not the first. Three: small, consistent adjustments create bigger changes than waiting until something breaks. Take care of your spine before it asks you to.

**"Myth: Adjustments Are Painful" (15s):**
> A lot of people think chiropractic adjustments are painful. But the truth is, most patients feel immediate relief. That crack you hear? It's just gas releasing from the joint. Completely normal.

**"Myth: Chiropractors Aren't Real Doctors" (15s):**
> Chiropractors complete a Doctor of Chiropractic degree. That's four years of graduate-level education focused on the spine, nervous system, and whole-body wellness. We're trained, licensed healthcare providers.

**"Myth: Once You Start, You Go Forever" (15s):**
> You don't have to do anything. We create a care plan based on your goals. Some people come in for a specific issue and graduate. Others choose ongoing wellness care. It's always your choice.

**"Why Kids Need Chiropractic" (20s):**
> Kids fall an average of 25 times a day when they're learning to walk. Those little impacts add up. Pediatric chiropractic is gentle and helps their growing spines develop properly. It's not about cracking. It's about growing strong.

**"The 30-Second Posture Check" (15s):**
> Quick posture check. Stand up. Roll your shoulders back. Tuck your chin slightly. Now imagine a string pulling the top of your head toward the ceiling. Feel that? That's where your body wants to be. Try it three times today.

**"What Happens at Your First Visit" (30s):**
> Your first visit is simple. We talk about what's going on, do a thorough exam, and take any needed images. Then we build a personalized plan just for you. No pressure, no surprises. Just a clear path to feeling your best.

## 2. Office Tour / First Impressions

Show your real space. Let people feel what it's like to walk in. AI makes the tour cinematic.

### FILM THIS:
- Walk through your front door with the phone (stabilized or on a gimbal)
- Slow, steady movement through reception, past the desk, down the hallway, into a treatment room
- Film 3-5 second segments at each location (don't try one continuous take)
- Capture detail shots: plants, clean surfaces, natural light, your logo on the wall
- Morning light is ideal

### ENHANCE WITH:

**VFX Trick 1 - Scene Extension:**
Film yourself walking through the real front door (3 seconds). Use the last frame as the AI start frame:
```
"Camera continues smoothly into a beautifully lit modern chiropractic
office. Golden morning light streams through large windows. Teal
accent walls, natural wood reception desk, fresh plants. Camera glides
past comfortable seating into a pristine treatment room. Cinematic
dolly movement, warm tones, shallow depth of field, 50mm lens."
```
The viewer can't tell where real footage ends and AI extension begins.

**VFX Trick 2 - Impossible Camera Moves:**
Film a steady static shot of your reception area or treatment room (5 seconds, tripod):
```
Cinema Studio: Upload clip > Apply "Slow Orbit" or "Crane Up and Over" preset
Model: Sora 2
```
Your static phone shot now looks like it was filmed on a cinema dolly.

**Mixed Media (all clips):**
```
Upload all real office clips > Apply Clean/Premium preset to every one
Primary Color: Teal (#0d9488)
```
Every clip in the tour gets the same polished, cohesive look.

## 3. Exercise & Posture Demos

You demonstrating real stretches, exercises, and posture corrections. This is inherently real content. AI makes it look professional.

### FILM THIS:
- Use a tripod or lean your phone against something stable
- Wear clean, solid-colored clothing (no busy patterns)
- Show the full movement from a side or 3/4 angle
- Demonstrate slowly and deliberately, 10-15 seconds per exercise
- Film against a clean background (even a plain wall works)

### ENHANCE WITH:

**VFX Trick 3 - Environment Enhancement:**
```
Mixed Media Settings:
- Preset: Dynamic Sport Ad (for exercise content)
- FPS: 60 (smooth movement)
- Primary Color: Teal
```
Your demo filmed against a plain wall now looks like it was shot in a professional wellness studio.

**Cinema Studio (for standout clips):**
Film a steady shot of yourself mid-stretch. Upload and apply "Slow Orbit" preset. The camera slowly circles you during the stretch, making it feel cinematic and engaging.

## 4. Patient Moments & Reactions

The most powerful content you can create. Real human connection that no AI can fake.

### FILM THIS:
- Post-adjustment reactions (genuine smiles, "wow that feels better")
- Greetings at the front desk (warmth, first-name basis)
- Milestone moments ("your 10th visit!", ringing a bell, a high-five)
- Candid conversations (not staged, just real moments of connection)
- **Always get consent before filming patients**

### ENHANCE WITH:

**Mixed Media (always):**
```
Preset: Clean/Premium (for professional moments)
   or Cinematic Mood (for emotional/testimonial moments)
Primary Color: Teal (#0d9488)
```

**VFX Trick 2 (for milestone moments):**
Film a steady shot of a patient ringing a bell or celebrating. Upload to Cinema Studio, apply "Crash Zoom" or "Slow Push In" for dramatic emphasis. A simple phone clip becomes a cinematic moment.

### Tip:
These clips are gold. Even 2-3 seconds of a genuine patient smile, enhanced with warm color grading, outperforms any AI-generated content. Prioritize capturing these above everything else.

## 5. Before & After / Transformation

Real posture progress. Real patient journeys. AI adds the visual storytelling.

### FILM THIS:
- Film yourself or a willing patient demonstrating "before" posture (slouched, forward head) - 3 seconds
- Film "after" posture (upright, aligned) - 3 seconds
- Same angle, same background, same framing for both
- Or: Film a real patient sharing how they feel now vs. when they started

### ENHANCE WITH:

**Mixed Media color contrast:**
- Process "before" clip with cool/muted color settings (lower warmth, slight desaturation)
- Process "after" clip with warm, vibrant settings (golden tones, full color)
- The color shift between before and after tells the transformation story visually

**VFX Trick 1 - Scene Extension (transition version):**
Film the "before" posture. Use the last frame as start frame for AI:
```
"Smooth morph transition. Lighting shifts from cool grey to warm
golden. The figure's posture gradually corrects, shoulders rolling
back, chin lifting. Warm, empowering lighting fills the frame."
```
Then cut to your real "after" clip for the authentic payoff.

## 6. Day in the Life

Multiple real clips from your actual day, stitched together with AI-enhanced transitions.

### FILM THIS (throughout the day, 3-5 seconds each):
1. Arriving at the office / unlocking the door
2. Turning on lights, prepping a room
3. Greeting the first patient
4. Reviewing notes or images
5. Performing an adjustment
6. Team moment (huddle, lunch, laugh)
7. End of day wrap-up

### ENHANCE WITH:

**Mixed Media (every clip, same preset):**
```
Upload ALL clips > Apply ONE consistent preset (Clean/Premium)
Primary Color: Teal (#0d9488)
```
This is the key: one preset across every clip makes the whole day feel like one cohesive production, not choppy phone footage.

**VFX Trick 1 - AI transitions between real clips:**
```
"Smooth cinematic transition. Camera moves through a bright hallway
with warm natural light, soft teal wall accents. Shallow depth of
field, gentle forward motion. 2 seconds."
```
Generate 2-3 of these transition clips and place them between your real footage segments.

## 7. Testimonials & Patient Stories

Real patients sharing real experiences. The most trust-building content possible.

### FILM THIS:
- Patient seated in a comfortable spot (not the adjustment table, somewhere relaxed)
- Natural window light from the side
- Phone on tripod at eye level, slightly off-center for a documentary feel
- Let them talk naturally. Don't script it. Prompt them with: "How were you feeling when you first came in?" and "How do you feel now?"
- 30-60 seconds of raw footage

### ENHANCE WITH:

**Mixed Media:**
```
Preset: Cinematic Mood (film-like depth and softness for emotional content)
Primary Color: Teal (#0d9488)
```

**VFX Trick 2 - Slow Push In:**
Upload the steadiest portion to Cinema Studio. Apply "Slow Push In" preset. The camera gradually tightens on their face as they share their story. Intimate, engaging, professional.

**Intercut with real office B-roll:**
Between testimonial segments, cut to enhanced real footage of the office, treatment rooms, or the patient arriving. All processed through the same Mixed Media preset for consistency.

## 10. YouTube Long-Form Structure

For 3-10 minute educational videos. The pattern: real you + AI graphics + enhanced B-roll + real you.

### FILM THIS:
- **Main segments:** You speaking to camera (16:9 horizontal) covering each point. 1-3 minutes per section.
- **B-roll:** Real office footage, equipment, demonstrations relevant to the topic
- **Intro:** You delivering a hook line on camera (first 10 seconds)
- **Outro:** You delivering CTA on camera ("subscribe, comment, share")

### ENHANCE WITH:

**Mixed Media (all real footage):** Same home preset, consistent look.

**Vibe Motion chapter cards** between sections (see Tier 2 below).

**AI supplemental B-roll** between talking-head segments (see Tier 3 below) for concepts you can't film.

**Assembly pattern:**
```
1. Real: You delivering intro hook (enhanced with Mixed Media)
2. Vibe Motion: Animated chapter title card
3. Real: You explaining point 1 (enhanced)
4. Real: Office B-roll illustrating the point (enhanced with Cinema Studio camera move)
5. Vibe Motion: Stat or infographic supporting the point
6. Real: You explaining point 2 (enhanced)
7. AI Supplemental: Spine visualization or abstract concept (only if you can't film it)
8. Real: You wrapping up with CTA (enhanced)
9. Vibe Motion: Outro card with subscribe prompt
```

## 11. YouTube Channel Branding

These are the exceptions where AI generation makes sense. You can't "film" a logo animation or animated title card.

### FILM THIS (real elements to incorporate):
- Your actual office exterior at golden hour (5 seconds, steady)
- Your real logo printed/displayed somewhere in the office
- You giving a confident look or nod to camera (2-3 seconds)

### ENHANCE WITH:

**Intro (5-8 seconds):**
Film your real office exterior. Use Scene Extension (Trick 1):
```
"Camera pushes smoothly from the exterior into the office interior.
Warm golden light, cinematic movement. Final frame holds steady with
space for logo overlay. Teal and cream tones."
```
Overlay your Vibe Motion logo animation on the final frame.

**Outro (8-12 seconds):**
```
Vibe Motion: "Soft bokeh lights in teal and gold, slowly shifting.
Clean frame with large areas for end screen elements. Subtle warm glow.
'Collective Family Chiropractic' in small text at bottom."
```
