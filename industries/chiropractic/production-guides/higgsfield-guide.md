# Film Real. Enhance with AI.
### A Content Production Guide for Collective Family Chiropractic

---

## The Philosophy

Your content is real. Your office is real. Your adjustments are real. Your patients are real. That authenticity is your biggest competitive advantage, and no AI can replicate it.

What AI can do is make your real content look like you hired a film crew. The camera movements get smoother. The lighting looks cinematic. The transitions feel seamless. The graphics look like a design agency built them. But the foundation is always you, your practice, and the real moments that happen every day.

**This is not about generating fake videos. This is about upgrading real ones.**

### Why This Approach Wins

- **Your competitors** post shaky phone clips or generic stock footage. You post cinematic real content that happens to look like a production team was involved.
- **Patients trust real.** An AI-generated office tour feels hollow. A real walkthrough of your actual space, enhanced with smooth camera movement and warm color grading, feels inviting and professional.
- **Pure AI still has a tell.** Even the best AI video can feel slightly uncanny. Grounding content in real footage eliminates that problem entirely.
- **You become the brand.** Dr. Harper's face, voice, and presence anchor every piece of content. AI is invisible. You are not.

### The Content Mix

| Type | % of Posts | What It Is |
|------|-----------|-----------|
| **Hybrid** | ~80% | Real footage you film, enhanced by AI (scene extensions, camera upgrades, visual polish, environment enhancement) |
| **AI Graphics** | ~15% | Animated stats, infographics, kinetic quotes, branded motion cards (Vibe Motion). These are graphics by nature, not pretending to be real video. |
| **AI Supplemental** | ~5% | Things you literally can't film: spine visualizations, nervous system animations, abstract B-roll inserts between real footage segments |

## What to Film: Your Real Content Library

Your phone is your camera. You don't need professional equipment. What you do need is a library of real footage clips that become the raw material for everything. Shoot these regularly, batch them, and enhance them throughout the week.

### The Weekly Shot List

Film these clips every week. Each one takes 5-15 seconds. Aim for 15-25 clips per session.

| Category | What to Capture | Tips |
|----------|----------------|------|
| **You on camera** | Speaking to camera (tips, myths, education), both candid and scripted | Look directly into the lens. Natural lighting from a window. Speak with confidence. |
| **Office atmosphere** | Reception area, treatment rooms, hallways, exterior, plants, details | Slow, steady movement. Morning light is best. Show the warmth of the space. |
| **Adjustments & treatment** | Real adjustments (with patient consent), table setup, hands at work | Steady camera on a surface or tripod. Capture the precision and care. |
| **Patient moments** | Greetings, smiles, post-adjustment reactions, conversations (with consent) | Candid is better than posed. Capture the genuine connection. |
| **Team & culture** | Team huddles, prep routines, candid laughs, high-fives, behind the scenes | These humanize your practice. Don't overthink them. |
| **Exercises & demos** | Stretches, posture corrections, ergonomic tips demonstrated in real time | Use a stable surface or tripod. Show the full movement clearly. |
| **Detail shots** | Spine models, equipment close-ups, hands sanitizing, table adjustments | These become powerful B-roll inserts between main clips. |

### How to Shoot for AI Enhancement

Your phone footage becomes the "plate" that AI builds on. The better the plate, the better the enhancement. Follow these guidelines:

| Factor | Do This | Avoid This |
|--------|---------|------------|
| **Lighting** | Natural window light at 45 degrees, or consistent overhead. Well-lit, even exposure. | Mixed sources, harsh shadows, backlighting that silhouettes you |
| **Stability** | Tripod, lean phone on a surface, or use a gimbal. Steady is critical. | Shaky handheld, jerky starts/stops |
| **Background** | Clean, uncluttered. Clear contrast between you and what's behind you. | Busy patterns, reflective surfaces, moving objects in background |
| **Framing** | Leave breathing room around the subject. Don't crop too tight. | Edge-to-edge framing with no space for AI to work with |
| **Exposure** | Lock it so it doesn't auto-adjust mid-clip. Slightly bright is better than dark. | Auto-exposure that shifts when you move |
| **Duration** | 3-15 seconds per clip. The sweet spot for AI processing. | Single long takes that can't be segmented |
| **Movement** | If panning, move slowly (~1-2 ft/sec). Smooth, deliberate. | Fast pans, sudden direction changes, rapid zooms |

### Batch Filming Strategy

Don't film one clip at a time throughout the week. Batch it:

1. **Pick one 30-45 minute window** (morning light is ideal)
2. **Shoot all "you on camera" clips first** (while energy is fresh)
3. **Walk the office** capturing atmosphere, detail shots, and room clips
4. **Capture any patient moments** during regular appointments (with consent)
5. **End with exercises/demos** if applicable that week
6. **Transfer to computer** and organize by category

This gives you 15-25 raw clips to enhance with AI throughout the week.

## How AI Enhances Your Real Footage

This is where Higgsfield comes in. Every technique below starts with real footage you already filmed and upgrades it.

### The 3 VFX Tricks

These are the core techniques filmmakers use to blend real shots with AI elements. Each one starts with your phone footage and ends with content that looks professionally produced.

#### Trick 1: Scene Extension

Film a real moment, then use AI to seamlessly continue the scene beyond what your camera captured. The viewer can't tell where real ends and AI begins.

**How it works:**
1. Film a real 3-5 second clip (e.g., patient walking through your door)
2. Extract the last frame of your clip
3. Use that frame as the "start frame" for AI generation
4. AI continues the scene with cinematic quality

**Examples for your practice:**
- Film a patient arriving at reception. AI extends into a cinematic glide through the treatment rooms.
- Film your hands beginning an adjustment. AI extends into a smooth visualization of the spine responding.
- Film yourself walking toward the camera. AI extends with a dramatic slow-mo approach shot.

**Higgsfield prompt for the extension:**
```
"Camera continues smoothly forward into a bright, warm treatment room.
Golden morning light streams through windows. Teal accent walls, clean
adjustment table, natural wood details. Cinematic dolly movement, warm
tones, shallow depth of field, 50mm lens."
```

#### Trick 2: Impossible Camera Moves

Take a simple static phone shot and add camera movements that would normally require a dolly, crane, or drone.

**How it works:**
1. Film a steady 3-5 second clip (tripod or phone leaned against something)
2. Upload to Higgsfield Cinema Studio
3. Apply a camera preset (Slow Orbit, Crane Up, Bullet Time, 360 Rotation, etc.)
4. Your static clip now has Hollywood-level camera movement

**Examples for your practice:**
- Static shot of your adjustment table becomes a slow cinematic orbit
- Simple hallway clip becomes a smooth Steadicam walkthrough
- Steady shot of you mid-demonstration becomes a dramatic Bullet Time freeze
- Reception desk shot becomes a slow crane-up reveal of the whole office

**This is the easiest trick to start with.** Film any steady shot, apply a camera preset, instant upgrade.

#### Trick 3: Environment Enhancement

Film yourself anywhere, and AI enhances or transforms the environment while preserving your real movements and expressions.

**How it works:**
1. Record yourself speaking or demonstrating against any background
2. Upload to Mixed Media
3. AI enhances the environment (better lighting, cleaner background, more professional feel)
4. Your real movements and expressions stay intact

**Examples for your practice:**
- Quick tip filmed in the break room gets warm, professional color grading and a cleaner background
- Exercise demo shot in a cluttered room becomes a clean, well-lit wellness space
- Every office clip gets the same consistent brand aesthetic (your "signature look")

### Mixed Media: Your Signature Look Tool

This is the tool you'll use most. It takes any real clip and applies a consistent visual style, making all your content look cohesive and professional.

**Setup (do this once):**
1. Pick your **"home preset"** - the one visual style that becomes your brand's look
   - *Clean/Premium*: Best for medical/professional (recommended as your default)
   - *Cinematic Mood*: Film-like depth and softness (good for emotional/testimonial content)
2. Set your **brand colors**: Primary teal (#0d9488), secondary cream/gold
3. Choose your **FPS default**: 30fps for most content, 60fps for exercise/movement clips

**Weekly workflow:**
- Upload all your raw clips from the batch filming session
- Apply your home preset to everything
- Adjust individual clips only if needed (FPS for fast movement, color for mood)
- Export enhanced versions

**The result:** Every piece of content from your practice has the same polished, professional feel, whether it was shot in the treatment room, the hallway, or the parking lot.

If your real footage has slight shakiness or inconsistent motion, Motion Engine equalizes the movement across all your clips. This prevents the subtle jitter that signals "phone footage" to the viewer's eye.

### Advanced Seamlessness Techniques

These techniques push Enhanced-style videos from "good enough" to truly invisible AI integration. Inspired by River Cody's approach of hiding AI inside real footage so viewers cannot distinguish between the two.

#### Color-Grade Matching

The most common tell when cutting between real and AI footage is mismatched color. After AI enhancement, match the color temperature, contrast, and saturation to your real footage. Export a still from your real clip as a reference frame. Apply that same warmth/tone to the AI output before assembling.

**The test:** Watch your edit on your phone at normal speed. If you notice a shift in warmth or contrast at any cut point, the color grade doesn't match. Fix it before posting.

#### Motion Tracking Overlays

Use AI to add elements (environmental details, text, subtle visual additions) that track motion in your real footage. The key is that added elements should follow natural camera movement, not float independently. This is how you add visual richness to a real shot without it looking composited.

**Example:** A slow pan across your treatment room. AI adds warm light rays through the window that track with the camera movement, making the space feel more inviting without any visible compositing.

#### Voice Clone + Lip Sync Workflow

For full_ai videos where "you" still need to appear speaking naturally:

1. Record your voiceover cleanly (quiet room, consistent mic distance)
2. Use Kling 2.6/3.0 or Lipsync Studio to generate synchronized lip movements on AI-generated talent shots
3. Match the lip-synced output to your voiceover timing
4. Color-grade the result to match any adjacent real footage

This enables full_ai production style videos that still feel personal and authentic.

#### The Seamlessness Checklist

Before publishing any Enhanced video, run through this:

1. Watch the full edit on your phone at 1x speed. Can you spot where real ends and AI begins?
2. Check cut points for color temperature shifts between real and AI shots
3. Verify any AI-added motion elements track naturally with camera movement
4. Confirm upscaled phone footage matches the resolution of AI segments
5. If voice cloning was used, listen for unnatural cadence or mismatched room tone

If anything feels off at normal viewing speed, fix it. The goal is zero visible seams.

## Where AI Generates from Scratch

Some content can't be filmed. For these specific cases, AI generation from scratch makes sense. But this should be ~5% of your total content, not the default.

### What to Generate (Not Film)

| Content | Why AI | Higgsfield Tool |
|---------|--------|----------------|
| **Spine/nervous system visualizations** | You can't film inside a body | Cinema Studio or Sora 2 |
| **Abstract wellness concepts** | Energy flow, stress patterns, healing processes | Cinema Studio |
| **Anatomical animations** | Vertebrae alignment, posture mechanics | Draw to Video |
| **Cinematic B-roll inserts** | Transitional footage between real clips (nature, abstract motion, light effects) | Sora 2 or Minimax |

### Prompt Tips for Supplemental AI Clips

Keep these prompts focused on what complements your real footage:

```
"A detailed 3D visualization of the cervical spine. Vertebrae glow in
soft teal light as they gently align. Camera slowly orbits. Clean dark
background, medical-professional quality, shallow depth of field."
```

```
"Abstract visualization of the nervous system branching from the spine
outward. Soft teal and white light on dark background. Gentle pulse of
energy traveling from brain through the spinal cord. Clean, calming,
educational tone."
```

These clips are inserts, not standalone content. They go between your real talking-head segments or as quick visual breaks in educational YouTube videos.

## Platform Optimization

### Instagram Reels
- **Duration:** 15-30 seconds (up to 60s for educational)
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical
- **Pacing:** Slower, cinematic cuts outperform rapid cuts
- **Hook:** First 1-2 seconds must grab attention (your face, a surprising movement, a bold text overlay)
- **Real footage anchor:** Every Reel should feature at least one real clip. Even a 2-second real shot at the open builds trust.

### YouTube Shorts
- **Duration:** Up to 60 seconds
- **Format:** 9:16 vertical
- **Approach:** Slightly longer and more educational than Reels
- **Repurpose:** Many Reels repost directly as Shorts

### YouTube Long-Form
- **Duration:** 3-10 minutes
- **Format:** 16:9 horizontal
- **Structure:** Real talking-head segments + Vibe Motion chapter cards + AI B-roll inserts + real office footage enhanced with Mixed Media
- **The pattern:** You speaking (real) > animated graphic (Vibe Motion) > B-roll of the concept (AI or enhanced real) > back to you speaking (real)

### Cross-Platform Consistency
> Maintain the same visual aesthetic across every platform. Your Mixed Media "home preset" is the key to this. When someone sees your content on IG, then finds your YouTube, it should feel like the same brand instantly.

## The Production Workflow

Filming comes first. AI comes second.

1. **Film** - Batch shoot 15-25 clips in one 30-45 min session
2. **Organize** - Sort clips by category (talking head, office, patient, exercise, detail)
3. **Enhance** - Run all clips through Mixed Media with your home preset
4. **Upgrade** - Apply Cinema Studio camera moves to the best static shots (Trick 2)
5. **Extend** - Use scene extension (Trick 1) on clips that need a cinematic continuation
6. **Generate graphics** - Create any Vibe Motion stat cards, quote cards, or chapter titles needed
7. **Generate supplemental** - Create any AI B-roll inserts needed (spine visualizations, abstract concepts)
8. **Assemble** - Combine enhanced real clips + graphics + supplemental into final posts
9. **Caption & schedule** - Add text overlays, captions, schedule across platforms

---

## Making the Blend Seamless

When your final content combines real footage, enhanced clips, AI extensions, and graphics, these finishing touches make it feel like one cohesive piece:

1. **Color match everything** - Same warmth, contrast, and saturation across all sources (your Mixed Media preset handles most of this)
2. **Match the grain** - If your real footage has any film grain, add subtle grain to AI clips too
3. **Sound continuity** - Natural room tone from real clips should carry through AI segments. Don't let the audio go silent during AI inserts.
4. **Transition with purpose** - Use movement, lighting shifts, or Vibe Motion graphic cards to bridge between real and AI segments. Don't hard-cut from real to AI.
5. **Upscale real footage** - If the quality gap between your phone clips and AI output is noticeable, run real clips through Upscale first
