**Platform:** YouTube (primary), Instagram, Podcast (Spotify/Apple)
**Followers:**
- YouTube: 1.3M subscribers
- Instagram: 143K followers, 5,646 posts
- Practice account (@myrejuve): 5,349 followers, 173 posts

**Niche:** Helping people "Inspire, Lead and Succeed" through active lifestyle and chiropractic care, focused on aligning spines and minds and connecting people with natural health.
**Analyzed:** 2026-03-05

---

## Overview

Dr. Jason Worrall has helped thousands of people recover from physical and mental trauma through specific, scientific chiropractic care, amassing over a million YouTube subscribers by delivering high-quality adjustments and his notably professional bedside manner. He recently relocated his practice from California to Miami, where he established Rejuve, a Corrective Chiropractic Wellness Center. His content strategy has evolved significantly, pivoting from patient-facing adjustment content toward a dual audience: chiropractic consumers AND other chiropractors he coaches on growing their practices.

---

## Top Performing Content

**1. Celebrity Adjustment Videos**
His patient roster includes high-profile celebrities such as Serena Williams, JuJu Smith-Schuster, Chuck Liddell, Tyron Woodley, Dwyane Wade, Eva Longoria, and Jason Statham. Videos featuring recognizable names drive outsized views. Hook type: **Social proof / aspirational identity hook.** Format: Long-form YouTube (Format D equivalent, talking head + procedural).

**2. "#AskDrJason" Q&A Series**
His content mix includes #AskDrJason episodes answering health, healing, and alignment questions, directly addressing audience pain points. Hook type: **Question Hook.** Format: Talking head with audience-sourced prompts (Format B equivalent).

**3. "#HealthIsWealth" Educational Episodes**
His #HealthIsWealth episodes cover all things healthy living, while #EQ101 episodes address emotional intelligence. Hook type: **Statement/philosophy hook.** Format: Monologue or interview (Format A/C equivalent).

**4. Adjustment Procedure Videos**
His channel focuses on inspiring his audience through his specialty in chiropractic care, with adjustment videos as the backbone of his viewership. Hook type: **Visual/ASMR hook.** Format: Over-the-shoulder procedural (Format E equivalent).

**5. Chiro Business / Coaching Content**
His current positioning on X/Twitter is "Chiropractic saved my life. Now I'm helping 1,000 Chiros grow past 30k/mo and learn to scale." Hook type: **Outcome hook / transformation hook.** Format: Talking head, LinkedIn text posts, reels.

## Content Patterns

- **Dual audience split.** Patient-facing adjustment content on YouTube coexists with B2B coaching content for other chiropractors on Instagram and LinkedIn. This is a notable brand fragmentation.
- **Personal origin story as evergreen anchor.** His passion for chiropractic care began after a life-altering car accident, and having found relief through chiropractic treatments, he enrolled in chiropractic school at 18. This story appears across all platforms and formats.
- **"High love practice" brand identity.** He describes his approach as "doctors don't really care, they're just going through the motions. I have a high love practice." This philosophy drives his tone and differentiates him from ASMR-only crack accounts.
- **Celebrity name-dropping as credibility signal.** Celebrity patients are referenced across nearly every media appearance and bio.
- **"DREAM" framework for health content.** He teaches principles of proper health under the acronym DREAM: Diet, Rest, Exercise, Alignment, and Mindset. This creates a repeatable content structure.
- **Multi-platform ecosystem.** He operates as an entrepreneur, chiropractor, public speaker, and vlogger, with a podcast mixing health content, Q&A, and emotional intelligence topics.

---

## Hook Preferences

- **Philosophy/lifestyle statement hooks.** "Health is on purpose." "Health IS Wealth." Opens with conviction before explanation.
- **Celebrity social proof hooks.** Patient name-drops in titles and thumbnails to trigger aspirational curiosity.
- **Personal origin story hooks.** Leads with his own car accident and transformation to establish trust and relatability.
- **Question hooks via #AskDrJason.** Pulls directly from audience questions, making the viewer feel seen before the video starts.
- **Identity alignment hooks.** "When you're body is functioning well, you have a much better chance at living at your highest version." Appeals to self-improvement motivation, not just pain relief.
- **Corrective care contrast hook.** Among many great chiropractors, he sets himself apart by focusing on corrective care, which means changing the spine to bring it to the ideal alignment. "Most chiropractors do X. I do Y." pattern.

## Key Takeaways

- **The personal origin story is his most powerful recurring asset.** Every platform leads with the car accident narrative. We can mirror this with Jordan's own origin story or patient transformation stories.
- **"Health IS Wealth" as a philosophy is highly shareable.** The hashtag/worldview creates emotional buy-in beyond pain relief. Collective Family Chiropractic can build an equivalent philosophy hook ("Whole family, whole health").
- **Audience fragmentation is a real risk.** DrJason's pivot to coaching other chiropractors dilutes his patient-facing credibility and creates mixed messaging. Collective Family Chiropractic has an opportunity to own the patient-facing space he is partially vacating.
- **Celebrity association builds aspirational pull.** Even without A-list patients, our practice can create aspirational content around athlete patients, prenatal moms, and pediatric milestones.
- **The "corrective care" framework is educationally compelling.** He uses the latest technology to measure spine alignment down to degrees and millimeters, then works through exercises, adjustments, and spinal remodeling to return the spine to optimal alignment, then teaches corrective exercises to maintain it. A similar framework for family wellness could drive strong save and share metrics.
- **He has almost no pediatric or prenatal content.** This is the clearest content gap we can exploit.

---

## Gaps (Opportunities for Us)

- **Zero pediatric/infant content.** His channel is adult and athlete-heavy. Newborn adjustments, toddler care, and school-age wellness are completely unaddressed in his catalog.
- **Zero prenatal/postpartum content.** A massive, highly engaged audience that DrJason has never targeted.
- **No family-as-a-unit framing.** He treats individuals. Collective Family Chiropractic can own "the whole family comes in together" as a visual and emotional story format nobody in his space is doing.
- **No humor or personality-driven short form.** His content is warm but earnest. Jordan's natural deadpan style is a format advantage DrJason doesn't have and can't easily replicate.
- **No "before and after posture" or milestone content for kids.** Content around a child's first adjustment, a baby's posture after delivery, or a pregnant mom's third-trimester relief are completely open territory.
- **No on-screen caption-driven educational reels.** His short-form repurposing is minimal. Strong graphic/text-overlay educational content is wide open.
