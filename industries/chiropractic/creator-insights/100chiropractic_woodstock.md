## Strengths (Learn From)

1. **Consistent franchise campaign participation.** They reliably execute national franchise seasonal campaigns (Back to You Challenge, Halloween Contest, Bundle of Love Giveaway), which creates a rhythm of posts even if local engagement is minimal. The campaign structure itself is worth noting: they always tie a giveaway to a patient testimonial submission, which surfaces social proof.

2. **Pop quiz / swipe carousel format.** They use this repeatedly: present a multiple-choice question on slide 1, answer on slide 2. The format creates a scroll-stop moment and invites comment interaction ("Comment A or B!"). It is low-production and replicable.

3. **Supplement cross-sell integration.** They weave their in-house nutrition line (100% Nutrition brand) into the content calendar regularly. This signals a monetization layer beyond adjustments. Worth noting as a category even if we don't replicate it directly.

4. **"Sports teams use chiropractic" social proof format.** Two posts used NFL/MLB endorsement angles ("All 32 NFL teams use chiropractic care"). These are evergreen authority-builders that do not require original research.

5. **Seasonal health tie-ins.** Posts like "How cold weather affects your joints" or "Holiday treats and your spine" connect relevant seasonal moments to chiropractic. The idea is sound even if their execution is flat and static.

---

## Gaps (What They're NOT Doing -- Our Opportunity)

1. **No doctor on camera.** Zero posts feature Dr. Sims or Dr. Criswell speaking directly to camera. There is no personality, no face, no voice. The entire account is branded graphics, stock imagery, and franchise templates. A single authentic doctor-on-camera Reel would immediately differentiate.

2. **No patient stories or testimonials (organic).** They run a giveaway to collect testimonials but never post patient stories as organic content. Format G (Patient Story) is entirely absent from their feed.

3. **No local Woodstock identity.** Nothing references the community, local events, Woodstock GA culture, or neighborhood context. "Woodstock" appears only as a hashtag. A locally rooted practice with real community ties has a massive opening here.

4. **No pediatric or prenatal content.** Their franchise page mentions pediatric and prenatal chiropractic as services, but their social content never addresses it. This is a significant audience gap for a family-oriented market.

5. **No exercise or movement-based content series.** One neck stretch video is in 50 posts. A consistent "exercise of the week" or "morning mobility" series using Format C is completely absent.

6. **No condition-specific education.** No posts address specific conditions (sciatica, headaches, tech neck, postural issues) with any depth. They post generic chiropractic facts, not condition-specific education that drives search and saves.

7. **No short-form Reels strategy.** Their video posts are rare and appear to be afterthoughts. Format F micro-content (6-15s, single fact, TikTok-style) does not exist in their library.

8. **No humor or personality.** Every post is earnest, corporate, and interchangeable with any other franchise location's content. Deadpan or self-aware humor would stand out instantly in this feed environment.

9. **No YouTube presence at all.** Zero long-form content. Educational depth on YouTube Shorts or long-form is a completely uncontested channel.
