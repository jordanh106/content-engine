## Overview

Ribley Family Chiropractic is the longest-established chiropractic practice in the Towne Lake area of Woodstock, GA, operating since 1999 under founders Drs. Dan and Kristin Ribley. The practice has expanded to 8 chiropractors and 7 massage therapists, making it one of the largest chiropractic groups in Cherokee County. Their Instagram presence is minimal for their practice size: 471 followers and 185 posts accumulated over the life of the account, posting approximately 1-2x per week with content that leans heavily toward practice announcements and promotional one-offs rather than systematic educational or entertainment-driven content. Dr. Palmer Ribley, a 3rd-generation chiropractor and Life University graduate (2020), runs a separate sub-brand, Sacred Alignment, from within the Ribley practice specifically for Y-strap content and decompression-focused patients.

## Patterns

### Hook Preferences

Ribley's hooks fall into two categories: **announcement-style** ("Exciting News," "This just in," "Look Ma!") and **direct question** ("Do you need a chiropractor?"). The announcement hooks are event-driven and generate one-time engagement rather than building a repeatable content pattern. The question hook is their most strategically sound format but appears to be used rarely. No educational hooks, myth-busting hooks, or pattern-interrupt hooks are visible in indexed content. Hooks do not appear to be tested or iterated.

### Format Preferences

The account skews toward:
- **Practice announcements** (new booking system, TV commercials, news features)
- **Office tours and behind-the-scenes** (Suite A walkthrough)
- **Observance/holiday posts** (International Women's Day)
- **Occasional patient testimonials** (screenshot-style or repost)

Educational Reels, condition-specific content, exercise demonstrations, myth-busting, and "what happens during an adjustment" walkthroughs are absent from indexed content. The content pattern suggests the account is managed reactively rather than from a content calendar.

### Posting Cadence

Approximately 1-2 posts per week. 185 posts across the account's full lifetime averages to roughly 1-2 per week if the account was opened around 2018-2019. No visible evidence of batch production, series, or themed content weeks. Posting appears opportunistic (when something happens at the practice) rather than strategic.

## Strengths (Learn From)

- **Practice scale signals trust**: 8 chiropractors and 7 massage therapists is a real social proof asset. Their "25+ Years" and "Voted Atlanta's Best Chiropractor 2024" awards carry weight that a newer practice cannot match. If they leaned into this more consistently it would be powerful.
- **TV commercial presence**: Getting a local TV commercial is a notable marketing investment that most chiropractic practices don't make. Their reels around the commercial reveal ("Grab your popcorn") show an instinct for entertainment even if the execution is limited.
- **Multi-practitioner diversity**: 8 doctors means 8 potential content personalities. Dr. Palmer Ribley's Y-strap sub-brand (Sacred Alignment) is the most content-ready angle in the entire practice, and he is leveraging it separately.
- **Broad service menu**: Massage therapy, nutrition, LifeWave X39 stem cell patches, and active therapies give them many content angles they are not using.

## Gaps (What They're NOT Doing, Our Opportunity)

1. **Zero condition-specific educational content.** No "what is sciatica," "signs you have a pinched nerve," "why your headaches are coming from your neck" type content. This is the highest-volume, highest-search-intent content category in chiropractic social media and it is completely absent.

2. **No exercise or movement content.** No demonstrations of stretches, rehab exercises, at-home relief techniques. This is the most shareable and save-driving content category and they produce none of it.

3. **No myth-busting.** "Does cracking your knuckles cause arthritis?", "Is chiropractic safe during pregnancy?", "Is cracking your own back bad?" are high-engagement formats with zero competition from this account.

4. **No pediatric chiropractic content.** They list pediatric care as a service on their website and have multiple female chiropractors. Mom-audience content (babies, kids, car seats, backpack posture, growing pains) is an entirely unoccupied lane for them despite being directly adjacent to their stated services.

5. **No prenatal/pregnancy chiropractic content.** Same gap as pediatric. Their service menu includes it; their content does not.

6. **No "meet the team" format series.** With 8 chiropractors, a "30 seconds with Dr. [Name]" recurring format would humanize the practice and differentiate individual doctors. Not happening.

7. **No Y-strap or adjustment reaction content.** Dr. Palmer Ribley is doing this under Sacred Alignment, but the main account (@ribleyfamilychiropractic) gets none of that viral adjustment content. Adjustment reaction content is among the highest-performing content on TikTok and IG for chiropractic practices.

8. **No patient journey or transformation content.** They post occasional testimonials but there is no produced patient story format with a defined narrative arc.

9. **No "what to expect at your first visit" content.** A practice this large with 25 years of history should own this format locally, but nothing indexed suggests they produce it.

10. **No consistency or series structure.** No recurring format, no named series, no weekly topic themes. Content is reactive and isolated, making it impossible to build an audience expectation loop.

11. **Minimal TikTok presence.** The TikTok presence found is a location tag page, not an active creator account. They are not competing in the highest-reach chiropractic content environment at all.

## Competitive Context

Ribley Family Chiropractic's social media presence is significantly underbuilt relative to their offline authority. They have 25 years of community trust, 8 practitioners, award recognition, and a TV commercial budget, but only 471 Instagram followers. This is a resource and strategy gap, not a quality gap.

Their strongest competitive asset online is Dr. Palmer Ribley's Sacred Alignment sub-brand, which captures Y-strap curiosity traffic that the main account does not benefit from. If Palmer ever integrates Sacred Alignment content into the main account feed, their reach could grow quickly.

**The opportunity for Collective Family Chiropractic:** Ribley owns the offline reputation. Collective can own the online educational voice for the Woodstock chiropractic audience. Every topic Ribley is not covering on Instagram (conditions, exercises, pediatric care, pregnancy, myth-busting, patient journeys) is a piece of digital real estate that Collective can occupy and rank for locally before Ribley notices the gap.

The content priority for Collective should be depth and specificity: condition-level education, Woodstock-specific messaging, and a consistent series structure that rewards followers for returning. None of that exists in the Ribley content footprint.
