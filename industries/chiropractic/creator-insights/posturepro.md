## Overview



Annette Verpillot is the founder of Posturepro, a health company specialized in restoring the brain-body connection. She is a Canadian entrepreneur, therapist, public speaker, and internationally recognized Posturologist who has developed some of the world's most advanced rehabilitation and injury prevention techniques, teaching a method called the Posturepro Method to professionals in various fields.

 

Posturepro's core mission centers on restoring the brain-body connection through some of the world's most advanced rehabilitation and injury prevention techniques.

 Rather than focusing on muscle-level fixes, 

Verpillot argues that professionals are taught to assess pain using a biomechanical model that overlooks the brain, which is the key controlling system for all movement, and by incorporating neuroscience into systematic testing protocols, she provides a blueprint for addressing the root cause of postural problems.



Content strategy across platforms centers on a distinctive "fix the source, not the symptom" framework. 

The Instagram bio positions Posturepro as "#1 Permanent posture correction for chronic pain relief," with the explicit CTA to "Fix the source, stop managing symptoms."

 The Posturepro Method identifies three primary neurological sensory inputs, 

specifically which sensory input (eyes, feet, jaw) is sending wrong signals to the brain, using eye movement techniques to unlock the vestibular system, and correcting jaw alignment to release the entire kinetic chain from head to pelvis through brainstem retraining.

 This three-system framework drives the majority of content.



Annette has worked with elite athletes and presented to professional sports teams including the New Jersey Devils and San Francisco 49ers, and has presented her methods at TEDx Montreal, Breakfast Television, ICI Television, and Collectively Beautiful.

 

Her recent collaboration with McGill University was presented at the Movementis 2018 Conference at Harvard University and at La Sorbonne University in Paris in 2022, and she is currently working with researchers at the University of Montreal and UQAM to test her approach on Parkinson's patients and children with Developmental Coordination Disorder.

 This academic credibility is woven into content positioning, lending authority to what might otherwise seem like fringe health claims.

## Content Patterns

- **Three-system framework drives everything.** 

All content maps back to the feet, eyes, and jaw as the three sensory inputs sending wrong signals to the brain.

 Every topic, whether tongue posture, foot mechanics, or eye exercises, is a gateway back to this system.

- **"Fix the source, not the symptom" is the anchor message.** 

While traditional therapy methods prioritize the musculoskeletal system, the Posturepro Method prioritizes the brain's communication with the musculoskeletal system, addressing the body as a whole, modifying faulty motor patterns and delivering immediate, long-lasting results.



- **Counterintuitive reveals are the primary scroll-stopper format.** 

Hooks like "your jaw could be the reason for your uneven shoulders" work because most people focus on their back, core, or feet when trying to improve posture, but few realize jaw alignment plays a crucial role in full-body stability.



- **Comment-to-DM funnels are actively used.** 

CTA language like "Comment 'FEET,' and we'll send you information" appears in TikTok content

, signaling an active lead-generation funnel layered on top of educational content.

- **Mewing/tongue posture content taps high-volume trend traffic.** 

Content tagged #tongueposture #mewing #jawline positions Posturepro alongside viral mewing content while maintaining a neuroscience frame rather than aesthetics.



- **Content repurposing across all platforms is heavy.** The same frameworks appear on TikTok, Instagram, Substack, a blog, LinkedIn, and podcast appearances, suggesting a hub-and-spoke content model with IG/TikTok as the distribution arms.

- **Practitioner-facing content runs parallel to consumer content.** 

Module-style content like "Stop treating the compensation" and "learn how imbalances in feet, eyes, or jaw create the postural distortions you keep manually correcting" is designed specifically to attract healthcare professionals.



- **Research and academic credibility are cited regularly.** 

Collaboration with McGill University was presented at Harvard University and La Sorbonne

, and peer-reviewed citations appear in blog content to validate claims.

## Hook Preferences

- **Identity-challenge hooks.** Example: 

"For many people, their tongue rests at the bottom of the mouth, pushing against the bottom teeth. Is this you?"

 Forces the viewer to self-assess immediately.

- **Counterintuitive body-part-to-symptom connections.** Example: 

"Your teeth could be the reason your neck hurts! Align your jaw, and your posture might just follow."

 Delivers a reveal that contradicts the viewer's existing mental model.

- **Consequence-stack hooks.** Example: 

"Your tongue shapes your face, jaw, and posture every day. If it sits too low, it narrows your palate, weakens your chin, and pushes your head forward."

 Stacks negative consequences to create urgency.

- **"Did You Know" neuroscience drops.** Example: 

"Did you know that simple lateral eye movements can boost brain function and improve posture? These movements don't just help with visual tracking, they also stimulate both brain hemispheres."



- **Problem-reframe with a non-obvious cause.** Example: 

"What Really Causes Duck Feet (Hint: It's Not Tight Muscles)."

 Pulls in anyone who has searched about the topic and assumed the wrong root cause.

- **Reply-to hooks for algorithmic amplification.** 

Replying to user comments like "@Mortis: Your teeth could be the reason your neck hurts!"

 uses TikTok's reply-video feature to extend reach into existing comment threads organically.

- **Social proof + scale hooks.** Example: 

"Trusted by 5,000+ Individuals Who Breathe Better!"

 used in product-adjacent content to build authority before the ask.

## Format Preferences

- **Format A (Explainer) is the workhorse.** The tongue posture TikTok, the eye movement blog/video, and the brain-body connection framework all follow a "here's what it is, here's why it matters, here's the fix" structure. Educational explainers are the primary discovery vehicle.

- **Format D (Myth Buster) drives the most engagement.** Counterintuitive hooks ("your jaw is causing your neck pain," "duck feet aren't about tight muscles") consistently frame content as correcting what the audience thought they knew, which drives comments and shares.

- **Format B (Checklist/Self-Test) is used for saves and shareability.** 

Content like "four quick tests to see if your TMJ could be causing postural imbalances" and "the jaw isn't just for chewing" gives the audience actionable self-assessment tools

, which drive saves.

- **Format F (Quick Tip) is used for TikTok micro-content.** Short, punchy frames like the tongue-shapes-your-face video and the foot CTA video follow the sub-15-second quick tip format with a CTA or save hook.

- **Format E (Walkthrough) appears in paid course/product context.** 

The CoursePack content walks through a complete PosturePro system assessment-to-correction, with each course targeting a specific neurological pathway.

 This format is reserved for the monetization layer.

## Key Takeaways

- **The "fix the source" positioning is ownable for us, too.** Chiropractic is uniquely suited to the "root cause, not symptom management" message. Posturepro owns this for posture; we can own it for spinal function, nervous system health, and whole-body wellness.

- **The three-sensory-system framework is a content engine.** Posturepro generates near-infinite content by linking one body part (jaw, foot, eye) to a surprising downstream effect. We can do the same: cervical spine to digestion, atlas to headaches, sacrum to pelvic floor.

- **Counterintuitive body-part-to-symptom hooks are the highest-performing format.** "Your jaw is why your neck hurts" gets 90K+ likes. We can build the same hook structure: "Your lower back pain is starting in your pelvis," or "Your headaches are coming from your upper cervical spine."

- **The "Is this you?" self-assessment hook drives immediate scroll-stops.** Posturepro consistently asks the audience to self-identify a problem. We can build this into every video: "Does your head tilt to one side in photos? Here's why."

- **Comment-to-DM funnels should be layered on educational content.** 

The "Comment 'FEET' and we'll send you information" approach

 is a proven conversion tool. We can use "Comment 'NECK' for a free posture guide" or "Comment 'KIDS' for our pediatric posture checklist."

- **Mewing/tongue posture is a crossover content opportunity.** 

Improper tongue posture can lead to sleep apnea, TMJD, vision problems, bad body posture, and tooth damage, and plenty can be done about it whether the patient is a child or an adult.

 This is a pediatric and family content angle we have not yet addressed.

- **Practitioner-facing content expands the audience and builds credibility.** 

Verpillot presents to professionals in medicine, chiropractic, dentistry, naturopathy, sports therapy, and ophthalmology.

 We can position Jordan as a trusted voice for parents, coaches, and teachers, not just patients.

- **Academic credibility claims earn trust fast.** Citing research (even brief references to published studies) elevates educational content. We can reference the research base behind chiropractic and neurology without losing audience accessibility.

- **Kids and developmental content is a Posturepro gap we can own completely.** 

Verpillot is currently testing her approach on children with Developmental Coordination Disorder

, but consumer-facing pediatric content is thin. Our prenatal and pediatric specialization fills this void directly.

- **The Posturepro Club membership model signals that this audience is hungry for ongoing education.** 

The membership promises monthly content, step-by-step guides, and daily routines for "proactive families committed to better health."

 This is our exact patient avatar, actively looking for credible guidance.

## Gaps (Opportunities for Us)

- **Zero prenatal content.** Posturepro does not address posture during pregnancy, pelvic floor changes, or postpartum recovery. This is our clearest differentiator and highest-priority gap to fill.

- **No pediatric patient-facing content.** 

Verpillot's pediatric work is research-based and not yet consumer-facing.

 We can own newborn, infant, toddler, and school-age posture content entirely.

- **No adjustment or chiropractic care content.** Posturepro positions around self-correction tools (insoles, mouthguards, courses). We offer something they cannot: hands-on adjusting care and in-office relationships. The human touch angle is untouched.

- **No family dynamic content.** Posturepro markets to individuals. We market to whole families coming in together. The "bring your whole family" content angle is completely unoccupied.

- **No school-age or screen-time content for parents.** 

Posturepro notes that too much screen time can lead to forward head posture and FHP strains the neck

, but does not produce parent-targeted content about kids' screen habits. We can own "tech neck in kids" content.

- **No sports or youth athlete content.** Posturepro's athlete work targets professionals and elite NCAA teams. Youth sports content (young athletes, weekend warriors) is wide open.

- **No warm or relatable tone.** Posturepro's content is authoritative and educational. It does not lean into warmth, humor, or community in the way a family practice can. Jordan's personality is a competitive advantage here.

- **No patient story or testimonial content visible.** Posturepro's social proof appears in product pages, not video content. Patient story format videos (Format G) are an untapped viral format in this space.
