# Production Plan: B1 - What Is Colic and How Can Chiropractic Help?

Date: 2026-03-06

## Hook Variations

- **Primary:** "If your baby cries for hours every day and nothing helps, here's what's actually happening." (Pattern: Question-adjacent statement, high emotional relevance, stops the scroll with specificity)
- **Variation A:** "Why does your baby cry for hours, no matter what you try?" (Pattern: "Why does your [subject] [symptom] every [time]?" - drives saves from exhausted parents bookmarking for later)
- **Variation B:** "Colic affects 1 in 5 babies. Most parents are never told what's causing it." (Pattern: Statistic + contrarian gap, drives shares from parents who feel validated by the information gap)

---

## Platform Optimization

- **Instagram Reels:** Lead with Shot 1 thumbnail framed tight on the mother's face. The emotional image does the work before the audio starts. Add closed captions in white with teal keyword highlights ("colic," "nervous system," "gentle adjustment"). Pin a comment with your booking link. This one saves well. Parents will send it to their partners.
- **TikTok:** The "lighter than the pressure you'd use to test a tomato" line is your organic share moment. If you stitch or duet bait, clip that line as a standalone. Add a text overlay on Shot 3 that reads "this gentle." Stitch-friendly ending: pause one beat before the final reassurance line so the emotional landing hits before the CTA.
- **YouTube Shorts:** Lean into the SEO title. "Colic and Chiropractic" is a high-intent search from exhausted parents at 2am, which is exactly who Shot 1 is showing. Add chapters in the description even for a 40s video. The statistic in the script ("more than 3 hours a day, more than 3 days a week, for more than 3 weeks") works well as a pinned comment for people who are trying to confirm whether their baby qualifies.
