# Production Plan: A1 - Sports Injury Recovery

Date: 2026-03-05

## Hook Variations

- **Primary:** "If you're only treating the painful spot, you're not actually treating the injury." (Pattern: Contrarian Statement)
- **Variation A:** "Why does fixing one injury sometimes create another one?" (Pattern: Question Hook / Why does [body part] [symptom])
- **Variation B:** "Most athletes treat the symptom. The injury keeps coming back. Here's why." (Pattern: Statistic/Contrarian setup with implied consequence)

---

## Platform Optimization

- **Instagram Reels:** Lead with Primary hook as on-screen text over Shot 1. Audience is visually primed for athlete content, so the knee grip image stops the scroll immediately. Add closed captions, especially for the kinetic chain explanation in the middle section. Save-worthy because of the "compensations create new problems" insight. End card should push to bio link for sports injury consultations.
- **TikTok:** Variation A hook performs well here. TikTok audiences respond to the question setup that promises a surprising answer. Keep captions tight and punchy, one phrase at a time. The anatomical visualization in Shot 2 is unusually high-production for TikTok and will stand out. Stitch and duet enabled to encourage reactions from other fitness creators.
- **YouTube Shorts:** Primary hook works best. YouTube Shorts rewards slightly longer payoff and this script delivers the "fix the system" line as a satisfying conclusion. Add chapter markers if repurposing to long-form. SEO-optimize the title toward "sports injury chiropractic" and "kinetic chain recovery." The educational depth here makes this the highest-save-potential platform for this video.
