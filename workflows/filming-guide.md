# Workflow: Film Real. Enhance with AI.

The hybrid approach: real footage enhanced with AI tools. This is the backbone of authentic content.

## When to Film Real

- Your face, your voice, your office, your patients/clients (with consent)
- Personality-driven content (behind the scenes, day in the life)
- Patient/client interactions and testimonials
- Adjustment/treatment/service demonstrations (real)
- Exercise demos (you performing them)

## When to Use AI Instead

- Historical or conceptual visualizations
- Anatomical or educational graphics
- Scenes requiring actors, locations, or props you don't have
- Scaling content production beyond filming capacity
- B-roll that would require a film crew

## The 80/20 Rule

Aim for approximately 80% real-footage content enhanced with AI, and 20% fully AI-generated content. The real footage builds personal connection. The AI content scales educational authority.

## Enhancement Techniques

### Higgsfield Mixed Media
Apply visual style presets to real footage for a consistent, polished look across all clips.

### Cinema Studio Camera Upgrades
Take raw phone footage and enhance it with cinematic camera characteristics (depth of field, color grading, lens character).

### Vibe Motion Graphics
Add animated text overlays, stat cards, and branded graphics to real footage.
