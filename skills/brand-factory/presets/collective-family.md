# Collective Family Chiropractic Brand Guidelines

## Brand Foundation

### Brand Archetype
**The Caregiver.** Brands that nurture, care, and protect. Every design decision should communicate trust, empathy, and commitment to well-being.

### Brand Values
- **Trust** - Earned through transparency, credentials, and consistency
- **Community-Focused** - Rooted in Woodstock, serving local families
- **Nurturing** - Gentle, supportive, protective
- **Clarity** - Simple, honest, jargon-free communication
- **Approachable** - Warm, welcoming, never clinical or intimidating

### Brand Voice
Empathetic, clear, and warm. Speak simply and honestly, avoiding medical jargon. Connect personally, like a supportive friend or family member offering gentle guidance.

### Brand Promise
Deeply personalized, gentle chiropractic care that understands and supports your family's unique wellness journey, making you feel heard, cared for, and empowered.

### Brand Mission
To empower families, expectant mothers, and children to achieve optimal health and well-being through specialized, gentle, and personalized chiropractic care that feels like family.

## Color Palette

### Primary Color
- **Calm Teal**: `#4C978D` - Primary brand color. Balance, tranquility, natural health. Use for primary buttons, links, section accents, and key UI elements.

### Secondary Colors
- **Soft Ivory**: `#F7F7F7` - Primary background. Clean, open, approachable.
- **Rich Charcoal**: `#232323` - Primary text. Strength and stability without harshness.
- **Warm Coral**: `#EC9A8F` - Accent color. Comfort, vitality, compassion. Use for highlights, CTAs, badges.
- **Deep Forest Green**: `#264A49` - Dark accent. Stability, depth, growth. Use for dark backgrounds.

### Color Usage Rules
- Primary buttons and interactive elements: Calm Teal `#4C978D`
- Destructive/alert actions: Warm Coral `#EC9A8F`
- Dark section backgrounds: Deep Forest Green `#264A49` with `#F7F7F7` text
- Subtle dividers and borders: `#E8E6DC` or slate-200
- Success/positive states: Calm Teal `#4C978D`

### Content Color (Video/Social)
- **Primary Teal**: `#0d9488` (slightly brighter for video overlays and social media)
- **Cream Background**: `#faf5ef`
- **Gold Accents**: For premium or highlight moments

## Typography

### Primary Font: Nunito Sans
Modern, friendly, highly legible. Slightly rounded shapes convey nurturing and approachable.
- **Headings**: 2.5rem - 3.5rem
- **Subheadings**: 1.5rem - 2rem
- **UI Labels**: 0.625rem (10px), font-black, uppercase, tracking-[0.2em]
- **Weights**: Light (300), Regular (400), SemiBold (600), Bold (700)

### Secondary Font: Georgia
Elegant, trustworthy serif. Warmth and traditional care. Use for body text and longer content.
- **Body Text**: 1rem - 1.15rem
- **Weights**: Regular (400), Bold (700)

### Video Typography
- Serif headings for authority and warmth
- Sans-serif body for readability on mobile
- Teal keyword highlights in captions

### Spacing and Shape
- Border radius: `rounded-2xl` for cards, `rounded-full` for buttons and badges
- Padding: `p-5` for cards, `px-4 py-2` for buttons
- Shadows: Subtle (`shadow-sm` or `shadow-slate-200/60`), never heavy drop shadows

## Content Rules

### Writing Style
- **Never use emdashes** in any content. Use commas, periods, or restructure.
- Refer to people as **"patients"** (not clients, members, or customers)
- Patient-facing content: warm, educational, empowering
- Practitioner-facing UI: clean, data-dense, actionable
- Avoid clinical jargon. Explain concepts in plain language.
- Use active voice. Be direct and clear.

### Humor and Personality
Jordan's natural humor style:
- **Deadpan asides** - Brief, dry commentary almost to yourself
- **Audience acknowledgment** - Saying what everyone is thinking
- **Relatable reactions** - Genuine awe or disbelief
- **Warm callbacks** - Humor that carries real admiration
- **CTA as comedy** - Subscribe asks are punchlines, not begs

Tone mix: 70% compelling information, 20% personality/humor, 10% actionable takeaways.

### Tone by Context
| Context | Tone |
|---------|------|
| Patient portal | Warm, personal, encouraging |
| Patient handbook | Educational, empowering, gentle |
| Dashboard/CRM | Professional, concise, actionable |
| Email | Friendly, clear, supportive |
| Social media video | Warm, educational, with natural humor |
| Error messages | Helpful, never blaming |

### Words to Use
nurture, support, gentle, wellness, family, growth, comfort, personalized, empower, trust, care, guide, journey, thrive, natural

### Words to Avoid
fix, broken, damage, aggressive, force, treatment plan (prefer "care plan"), compliance (prefer "progress"), side effects
