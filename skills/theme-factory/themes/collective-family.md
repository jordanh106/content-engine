# Collective Family

A warm, nurturing healthcare theme inspired by the brand identity of Collective Family Chiropractic. Designed to communicate trust, care, and approachability for family wellness content.

## Color Palette

- **Deep Forest Green**: `#264A49` - Primary dark background, header strips, contrast sections
- **Calm Teal**: `#4C978D` - Primary accent color, buttons, links, section highlights
- **Warm Coral**: `#EC9A8F` - Secondary accent for callouts, CTAs, warmth highlights
- **Soft Ivory**: `#F7F7F7` - Light backgrounds, content areas
- **Rich Charcoal**: `#232323` - Primary text color, headings

## Typography

- **Headers**: Nunito Sans Bold
- **Body Text**: Georgia

## Best Used For

Patient handbooks, care plans, practice reports, portal content, wellness guides, family healthcare documents, and any Collective Family Chiropractic branded material. This is the **default theme** for this project.
